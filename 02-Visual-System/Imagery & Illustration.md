# Imagery and illustration

Owns asset purpose, composition, accessibility and production use.

Use imagery when it communicates product content, context, explanation or relevant character. An empty state does not require illustration. A restrained explanation and useful action can be stronger than decorative art.

Inspect the project's asset language: crop, perspective, detail, palette, tone, proportions and quality. Do not assign flat/isometric/3D styles automatically by industry. Different campaign assets can coexist where the brand defines the relationship.

Keep the subject visible at each crop/width, reserve stable dimensions, define loading/failure/fallback and optimize formats/resolution. Avatar fallbacks must handle missing names/images without exposing sensitive identity.

Informative images need purpose-based alternative text; decorative images are excluded from assistive reading. Media with essential information requires accessible equivalents. [Accessibility](../01-Foundation/Accessibility.md)

Record provenance/license/usage rights for reused assets. AI-generated assets receive the same scrutiny for accuracy, artifacts, representation and fit; they do not define the brand by default.

Verify no distortion, no cropped critical information, readable overlays, fallback, performance expectations and export intent. Component controls belong in [Media Components](../05-Components/Media%20Components.md).
