# Art direction and composition

Owns visual intent and refined execution. Start from user task, information hierarchy, product character and existing design language. Inspiration is subordinate to [source precedence](../00-Core/AI%20Operating%20Rules.md).

For substantial visual work define the intended character with concrete decisions: typography contrast, composition, density, alignment, proportion, surface treatment, imagery and motion. Explain how each supports the task. "Modern," "clean" and "professional" do not specify a direction.

## Prevent automatic formulas

Do not default to large rounded rectangles, excessive or nested cards, gratuitous gradients, purple-blue palettes, glows, pills, oversized heroes, excessive whitespace, identical radii, decorative charts, random glass, generic sidebar/topbar dashboards, unnecessary shadows, floating containers, icon boxes or centered layouts. None is inherently prohibited; each needs a structural, brand or interaction reason.

Choose the composition before surface styling. A ledger favors alignment/comparison, messaging favors continuity, a creative tool favors workspace relationships, healthcare favors task safety, checkout favors review/commitment, and editorial content favors reading rhythm.

## Refine

- Inspect spacing rhythm, optical alignment, type rendering, icon weight, edge conditions and meaningful focal relationships.
- Review real data and content extremes before polishing static examples.
- Use restrained variation to show hierarchy; consistency of controls does not demand identical page composition.
- Make motion and imagery earn attention; remove effects that compete with information.
- Compare plausible directions where the brief permits; vary structure and emphasis, not just color.

Use the [critique rubric](../09-QA/Design%20Critique.md). A distinctive design needs a defensible product reason; an attractive decorative screen with poor recovery cannot pass.
