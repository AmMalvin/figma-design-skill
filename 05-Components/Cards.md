# Cards

Use a card when a bounded object needs its own preview, action or selection. Prefer rows, a list, a section or a table when continuous reading/comparison matters more than object boundaries.

Different product, pricing and analytical compositions may share a surface primitive without becoming variants of one universal card. Separate business data from core styling; avoid nested cards solely for hierarchy.

Identify title, key content, metadata and actions. Content grows/wraps predictably; media reserves dimensions and has fallback. Fixed equal heights require a scanning reason and accessible access to full content.

For a destination card use one clear link. Do not nest buttons inside another interactive element or make the whole surface intercept unrelated actions. Selection cards use a genuine choice control and state; informational cards do not get fake interactive hover.

Specify long title, missing image, loading/failure, selected when applicable and collection behavior. Avoid truncated identity/prices whose full meaning is inaccessible.

Check group boundaries, reading/focus order, touch actions and narrow reflow. [Component Principles](Component%20Principles.md), [Visual Hierarchy](../01-Foundation/Visual%20Hierarchy.md), [Accessibility](../01-Foundation/Accessibility.md)
