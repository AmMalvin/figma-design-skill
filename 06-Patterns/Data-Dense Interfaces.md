# Data-dense interfaces

Owns enterprise dashboards, analytics and operational workspaces.

Identify the decision: monitor exceptions, compare records, investigate, reconcile or take action. Choose summary, aligned table, chart, detail pane or multi-pane workspace accordingly. A metric tile grid is not the default.

Define data hierarchy, units, freshness, period, filter scope, access, provenance and confidence. Operational priority may favor incidents over headline revenue. A chart earns space by exposing a relationship relevant to action.

Set density for expertise, input and comparison; retain readable type, meaningful grouping and comfortable targets. Use less surface decoration to reduce scanning cost rather than simply removing needed data.

Keep filters/query/selection and row context across drill-down/back. Saved views/customization need discoverable reset and explain current scope. Batch actions show eligibility/count/partial outcomes.

Handle huge datasets, stale/partial data, service degradation, no matches, denied access and background refresh without stealing focus or erasing work. Pagination/virtualization choices require backend and accessible navigation evidence.

On narrow screens prioritize the task and provide explicit detail/scroll strategy; do not just stack every chart/card. Financial comparison, activity monitoring and content discovery require distinct structures.

Verify with actual dense and extreme data, multiple priorities, keyboard, touch and short/narrow windows.
