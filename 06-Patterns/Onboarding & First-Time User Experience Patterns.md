# Onboarding and first value

Canonical guide; Onboarding Patterns.md preserves the old route.

Identify the user's first meaningful outcome, existing expertise, invitation/entry context and prerequisites. Remove setup that can wait. Measure successful first value and retention/task guardrails rather than tour completion alone.

Choose contextual help, starter example, checklist, setup sequence or self-guided use from uncertainty. Optional tours are skippable and recoverable; they do not block experienced/invited users. Teach through a real task where possible.

Sample data is clearly distinguished from real records, removable and safe from accidental publication/payment. Starter templates reduce effort without imposing one composition on every product.

Permission prompts appear at the task where value is understandable, honor decline and provide a functional fallback/settings route where supported. Do not repeatedly demand notification consent.

Progress is truthful and persists only where supported. Handle skipped/optional steps, interruption, changing requirements, denied access, loading/failure and resume. Do not invent feature unlocks to manufacture engagement.

Guidance must survive keyboard, assistive reading, long content and narrow viewport; coach marks cannot be the sole way to discover critical actions. Avoid repetitive motion and focus theft.

Compare an administrator provisioning a team, an invited member joining an existing workspace and a consumer completing a first transfer: prerequisites and density differ even with shared controls.
