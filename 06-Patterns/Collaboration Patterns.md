# Collaboration

Owns shared-work awareness and conflict/review orchestration.

Identify participants, resource, effective permissions, shared actions and authoritative version. Presence is an estimate, not proof someone saved work. Do not expose private identity/activity through presence.

Keep comments/mentions attached to context; define ordering, edit/delete, resolve/reopen and notification consequences. Deep threads need readable navigation, not endless nesting. Live updates preserve focus, local draft and reading position.

Concurrent changes distinguish local/pending/committed/conflicting state. Preserve local work, offer compare/merge/reload and avoid last-write-wins claims unless policy supports them.

Invitations show resource, role/scope, pending/accepted/expired/revoked and recipient's resulting access. Sharing must show inherited/effective access and public-link implications; UI is not server authorization.

Approval records identify who, what version/scope and when. Changed content may invalidate a prior approval; stale approvals cannot imply current authorization. Recovery/ownership escalation follows real policy.

Notify only relevant participants; rate/group updates to avoid interrupting ongoing work. Provide meaningful announcements without narrating every live cursor.

Verify offline/reconnect if supported, permission revocation mid-task, concurrent edits, long discussions, narrow layout and keyboard.
