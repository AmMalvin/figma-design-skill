# Reference entry

Use one record conforming to [catalog.schema.json](../references/catalog.schema.json) in the shared inbox or catalog.

Provide source_name, url or local source, product_studio, platform, screen_flow, pattern_category, categories, authority_class, why_reference_matters, qualities_to_study, interaction_principles, visual_principles, ux_principles, do_not_copy, adaptation_to_current_system, reviewed_date, checked_date, status and evidence_limits.

For pending entries, record the user's question and leave extracted principle arrays empty. During review, attach observed scope and distinguish inference. Adaptation may be a rule awaiting project bindings; do not invent a project palette. Mark rejected/unavailable with a reason. See [intake workflow](../references/Reference%20Intake.md).
