# Design brief

- User and task: role, expertise, context and desired outcome.
- Product evidence: facts, assumptions, unknowns and source dates.
- Existing system: library/components, semantic tokens, brand/content and constraints.
- Scope: deliverable, entry/exit, supported platforms and acceptance conditions.
- Information priorities: first decision, next decision, secondary context.
- Risks: failure, permission, destructive action, latency and recovery.
- References: record IDs, accessible scope and question each source answers.
- Success evidence: observation or metric that would support the proposal.

Fill only relevant fields. Ask about missing information when it materially changes the interaction or scope; proceed with reversible assumptions and label them.
