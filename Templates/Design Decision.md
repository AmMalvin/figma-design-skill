# Design decision

## Outcome and rationale

State who does what, information priority and the interaction model. Explain why it fits the task and the meaningful trade-off.

## Alternatives and boundaries

Compare only plausible alternatives. State where the chosen pattern fails and what would replace it.

## Behavior and system mapping

Record relevant entry, transitions, success, failure, loading, empty, permission and interruption behavior. Explain responsive/long-content, keyboard, touch and assistive-technology intent. Map reuse to existing components and semantic tokens; keep feature-only compositions local.

## Evidence and review

Cite facts and reference principles; label assumptions. Report critique findings, implemented revisions and checks actually performed. List consequential unresolved decisions and the evidence needed to resolve them. Use [Validation Engine](../00-Core/Validation%20Engine.md).
