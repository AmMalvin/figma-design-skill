# Design research

Owns evidence gathering, synthesis, competitive review and usability studies. Do not fabricate participants, quotes, personas, findings or confidence.

| Uncertainty | Useful method | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Why does the task fail in context? | Interview plus observation | Preference alone does not show performance |
| Can users locate a category? | Card sorting for language; tree testing for findability | A card sort does not establish final navigation |
| Can users complete the proposed task? | Task-based usability test | Prototype results may hide latency and accessibility limits |
| How common is an issue? | Analytics, support evidence, appropriately sampled survey | Instrumentation and sampling bias need inspection |
| Which production model fits? | [Reference Intake](../references/Reference%20Intake.md) and competitive review | Popularity is not proof for this audience |

Define question, participants/context, method, task/material and success/failure evidence. Include relevant expertise, device and accessibility differences. Store only authorized, consented evidence and minimize personal information.

Synthesize observation -> interpretation -> opportunity -> decision. Link raw evidence where possible, retain counterexamples and flag inferred motives. A provisional persona is a task/context hypothesis, not a fictional biography presented as research.

Use realistic uncoached tasks. Record success, critical error, abandonment, recovery, assistance, time when meaningful and participant confidence. Include failure, empty/denied states and relevant widths/input methods. Summarize findings by severity and next change.

Choose [prototype fidelity](../04-Figma/Prototype%20Strategy.md) to expose uncertainty. If participants/runtime are unavailable, deliver the protocol and mark it unrun. A heuristic review has a separate evidence level.

Competitive review compares goal, information order, interaction, states, access, platform and system adaptation. Extract principles without copying branding or pixels.
