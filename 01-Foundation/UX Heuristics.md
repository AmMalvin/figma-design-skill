# UX heuristics

Use heuristics to diagnose observed friction, not to claim user-tested effectiveness. The [NN/G heuristics](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/) supply a general evaluation lens.

Inspect task status, user language, control/exit, familiar conventions, prevention of high-cost errors, memory demands, expert efficiency, relevant information, recovery and contextual help.

Record task/state, evidence, consequence, severity, proposed fix and trade-off. Investigate intentional deviations rather than mechanically counting violations.

A dense comparison table can outperform sparse cards for reconciliation. Minimalism should not remove necessary context; a repeated confirmation without new consequence can slow safe work.
