# Systems thinking

Owns boundaries, dependencies and downstream effects.

Identify changed scope and consumers: instances, aliases, code APIs, workflows, content, access, flags, localization, drafts, analytics and supported themes.

Separate shared contracts from business policy. Generic dialog behavior can be core; invoice approval policy belongs to the product. Reuse should reduce coupling.

Inspect callers and migration before change. New control dimensions affect alignment, targets, wrapping and overlays. Equal token values can serve different semantics; equality alone does not justify merging.

Fix shared defects at their owner when they recur. Do not invent future brands, modes or workflows without demand.

Deliver a proportional impact map and checks. [System Governance](../10-Governance/System%20Governance.md) owns lifecycle; [Token Architecture](../03-Design-Tokens/Token%20Architecture.md) owns aliases.
