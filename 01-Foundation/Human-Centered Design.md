# Human-centered design

Use as the context/inclusion lens. Describe users by task, expertise, constraints and environment, not a fictional average.

Consider attention, interruption, language, input, assistive technology, emotional stakes and network when they affect the task. Observe behavior before assuming stated preference explains a need.

Support autonomy with transparent defaults, consent, exit and recovery. Business objectives do not justify hidden costs, coerced notifications or obstructed cancellation.

Label inferred emotional/social needs. An anxious financial user may need precise pending status more than celebration; an expert analyst may need stable dense data more than tutorials.

Evidence methods live in [Design Research](Design%20Research.md); journeys in [User Journey Mapping](User%20Journey%20Mapping.md); requirements in [Accessibility](Accessibility.md). Apply relevant branches without duplicating their process.
