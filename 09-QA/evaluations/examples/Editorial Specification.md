# Editorial specification

Fixture: a policy knowledge library. Existing article typography, link roles and document metadata. Intended specification only.

## Information and reading model

Readers need to locate the applicable policy and trust that it is current. Provide category browsing and scoped search, then a document page with title, ownership, version/effective date and article body. A section outline helps long material; related documents follow or sit secondarily alongside reading.

Use editorial measure and hierarchy from the existing system. Do not apply dashboard tiles to each section. A brief policy may need no outline; a highly structured comparison task could instead use a decision table. Structure follows reading and retrieval needs.

## State and navigation

An archived link exposes its status and a route to the current policy. A missing document, access restriction and temporary load failure have distinct explanations. Returning to results restores query, filters and reading position where supported. Search suggestions must not reveal unauthorized policy titles.

Long headings wrap. Code, legal quotations or complex data tables may need a localized overflow treatment while the article itself reflows. Provide meaningful links, heading order and an accessible outline; do not hide important source metadata at small widths.

## System, content and handoff

Use existing article, link and metadata components. Keep title, owner, locale, current-version link and effective date as distinct content fields. Do not invent authoritative policy text. Define server permissions and archival redirects explicitly.

Motion is unnecessary for comprehension. If section navigation animates, respect reduced motion and keep focus/URL context meaningful. Loading should avoid presenting an absent policy as an empty library.

## Critique and limits

The proposed structure supports trust and reading, with a deliberate trade-off against visual novelty. Originality comes from useful provenance and retrieval rather than added decoration. Verify actual text, RTL, zoom, line breaks, contrast, heading semantics and archived routes in rendered/runtime artifacts. No usability or aesthetic quality claim is measured here.
